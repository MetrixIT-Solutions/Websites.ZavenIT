WORKING PROCESS

Our Working Process - How We Work For Our Customers

1

Discovery

We want to hear from you and understand enough about your business to be able to provide you with the best solution. We'll be taking notes and asking questions.

2

Planning

So that we're more aware of what we are doing, and we move on to architecting and producing the best solution to meet the demands of all project stakeholders.

3

Execute

Our creative team will go over your product numerous times for thorough testing and fine-tuning before it is ready to launch. When it's finished, however, you can be sure that it will be all you've been hoping for and then some.

4

Deliver

As soon as the software has been developed, tested, and received the client's approval, we will put it into operation. To ensure that the solution satisfies user expectations, the Zaven team will conduct user acceptance testing.
